# M11 review: the independent audit

**Scope:** the whole package at 8a03e95, by auditors who did not write the code, one per
angle of the implementation plan: the estimators and couplings against the papers; the
engine, its random streams, the pools and the sampler's pipeline; numerical robustness;
the API and its errors; and the tests' power, measured with mutants at d73a986 (the same
`src/`). Each finding was confirmed by a run, or by a reading against the spec, before it
was fixed. Three fixers, one per group of files, worked in separate worktrees; every line
they wrote was read before commit. A sixth auditor then read the documentation against
the code.

**Diff:** 3c029e8..HEAD.

## Outcome

### Results, estimators and diagnostics (508bf46)

- **`var`, `std` and `cov` lost all precision far from the origin** (estimators and
  numerics audits). They formed second moments about 0, so a posterior at 2.4e6 with a
  spread of 1e-4 gave a negative variance. They now use moments about the atoms' mean,
  which leaves the estimators unchanged since each replicate's weights sum to 1; a test
  at an offset of 1e8.
- **A test function that wrote to its input corrupted the result**, and every later
  estimate of its sampler (API audit). `h` now receives read-only atoms.
- NaN quantile levels and invalid estimator settings are refused, and the bounds validate
  their arguments.
- Hill's estimate is undefined when ties at its threshold leave the tail unresolved, and
  the variance share ignores spreads of rounding: both raised false warnings (numerics
  audit).
- The TV bound at k takes memory in proportion to R, not to the largest meeting time. The
  W1 bound's default times reach a censored pair's last distance, and its docstring says
  that with censored pairs it is a lower bound. Plots of pairs that all met at the lag
  skip the log scale. `merge` refuses runs of different kernels or initial distributions.
- The default histogram range comes from the atoms, which biases the outer bins by O(1/R)
  (z = 4.5 at R = 3, estimators audit); the docstring and the spec now say that a
  histogram is unbiased for a range fixed in advance. The `unbiased_covariance` docstring
  had a sign error.

### The engine, the sampler and the pools (fd3c1ef, f79a043)

- **Settings that were not integers gave wrong results without an error:**
  `engine.run(k=5.5)` stored no atoms, and `m=20.5` gave a mean 5 standard errors off; a
  float or NumPy `thin` crashed after tuning. Settings are converted with
  `operator.index`, and the sampler checks every given setting before tuning (DECISIONS
  #44).
- **The first Ctrl-C on a user's process pool lost the whole run:** SIGINT reaches the
  pool's idle workers, which die and fail every pending chunk. After the first Ctrl-C, a
  chunk failed that way counts as not run, and the guide shows the initializer that keeps
  such workers alive (DECISIONS #45).
- A run after a short pilot completes it to 100 pairs (DECISIONS #46). A kernel of the
  wrong dimension is refused at construction. The memory estimate counts the distances
  and no longer builds the averaging set. Pilot warnings say "pilot pair". The pickling
  error names its cause. `rng`, `pool` and foreign files get errors that name them.
- `names` must be distinct strings, one per coordinate: integer names made the summary
  fail when printed, repeated names lost parameters, and a bare string was split into
  letters.

### Kernels, couplings and tuning (d0d9120)

- **Tuning depended on the coordinates' scales.** Ridges from the mean variance and the
  trace made `sample()` choose k = 7,630 instead of 22 at an sd ratio of 1e8; at 1e10 its
  pilot failed, and at 1e12 tuning raised. The ridges are now per coordinate, and tuning
  is identical at sd ratios 1, 1e8, 1e10 and 1e16 (DECISIONS #47).
- The symmetry test refused matrices symmetric up to rounding (DECISIONS #47).
- The gradient was evaluated outside the support, where a user's gradient may raise
  (DECISIONS #49).
- Gibbs conditionals with array parameters crashed both couplings at the first coupled
  step, and a singular CRN covariance crashed the coupled step; a CRN conditional that is
  not normal would get the wrong law, which the docs now state (DECISIONS #48). A NaN
  log-density in the maximal coupling gave an identical draw or an endless loop, and a
  conditional's non-finite draw spread through the sweep; both now raise.
- The maximal coupling's first test is strict, as the implementation plan's convention
  asks. An infinite drift difference no longer freezes Y in the reflection coupling.
  `discrete_maximal` refuses rows without a finite maximum.
- The initial distributions validate finiteness and shapes; draws of shape (1, d), as
  from a Dirichlet, are points. `Kernel.tune` accepts every initial form that `sample`
  does and checks the target before its pilot. `Independence` takes any proposal with
  `sample` and `log_density`. A target function that returns `None` is named.

### Error types (84dd4e5)

The fixers had chosen `TypeError` for the run settings and `ValueError` for kernel
parameters and sizes. Every public number argument now raises `TypeError` for a wrong
type and `ValueError` for a value out of bounds, through two helpers (DECISIONS #50).

### The tests (518092f)

- **Mutation audit:** of 66 mutants at d73a986, 40 were killed by the fast suite and 26
  survived. Rerun at 84dd4e5, after the fixes above, 24 of them still survived, two after
  porting to moved code; tests added by the fixers killed two (the memory threshold, the
  TV bound at k), and one more fell to an unrelated warning. Survivors included a
  tolerance, or a single equal coordinate, counting as a meeting; the plug-in variance;
  the tail index without the lag shift; a wrong central interval; the cost of a censored
  pair; Hill's threshold; R-hat's between-chain variance; `choose_k` at other pilot sizes;
  a strict acceptance test, which the implementation plan's conventions require; and the
  default random-walk scale. New or changed tests kill all but one, each checked to pass
  on the code and fail under its mutant. The exception is the late warning at exactly a
  quarter (below).
- The pandas test was skipped everywhere; pandas joins the test group (DECISIONS #51).
- Under one mutant a parallel test ran for 8 min 49 s: CI jobs have timeouts, and the
  parallel tests' shared runs a cap of 10⁴ iterations. An engine test whose bound failed
  for 5.6% of seeds runs 30 replicates.

### The documentation against the code (90902fd, 1f21d20)

Ten findings, all fixed:

- **`merge` compared `str(kernel)` and `str(initial)`,** the fix of an engine finding
  above. A kernel's description omits its covariance, so runs of `RandomWalk(I)` and
  `RandomWalk(4I)` merged; a frozen SciPy distribution prints its address, so equal runs
  from two processes, the use of `merge`, were refused. `merge` checks what the results
  identify: seeds, settings, dimensions and disjoint replicates (DECISIONS #53).
- The error convention was overstated: `hill`, `w1_bound`, `slow_pair_threshold`,
  `CouplingReport.passed` and the `dims` of histograms took arguments of the wrong type
  without a named error. They now check them, and the spec says that NumPy converts
  array arguments.
- Getting started called the standard error unbiased; a tutorial linked the evaluation
  counts to the wrong page; the targets guide missed draws of shape (1, d); the engine
  docstring and the changelog claimed bitwise lane invariance for vectorised targets; the
  changelog credited the engine with a warning and the W1 bound with the meeting times;
  the pilot's cost missed its first evaluations; the spec's footer fraction left out
  censored pairs; and the Gibbs docstring gave SciPy's multivariate t, which has no
  `mean` or `cov`, as a conditional that common random numbers would mistreat.

### Performance

`sample()`'s 64 lanes left 36 of its 100 default replicates for a second wave. The engine
now advances 256 by default and never more than it was given, which ran the main run 1.23
to 1.57 times faster at 100 replicates and 1.27 to 2.31 times faster at 1,000; pool chunks
keep their size (DECISIONS #52).

## Answered, not changed

- **Objects of the wrong type** (API audit): `Mixture([..., "x"])`, `merge("x")` and
  `issues("x")` raise Python's `AttributeError`. Checking every object argument's type
  would add code without catching a wrong result; number arguments, whose wrong types did
  give wrong results, are checked (DECISIONS #44, #50).
- **`plotting.corner(bins=0)`** gives NumPy's message, which names `bins`.
  **`plotting.paths(result)`** takes the replicates of a run with `keep_paths=True`, as
  its docstring says.
- **A Student-t proposal draws df normals per point** (numerics audit): 513 MB at
  df = 10⁵ with 64 lanes. The tuned proposal has df = 5, and `df=None` gives the Gaussian.
- **The late warning at exactly a quarter** (mutation audit, D05): a boundary of no
  consequence, left without a test.
- **The spec example's test fails if its warnings change with the random streams**
  (mutation audit): it is deterministic, 2 of 15 other seeds warn, and a change of streams
  that makes it warn deserves a look.
- **Hypothesis writes `.hypothesis/`** in the working directory, which is the repository
  when the tests run as documented.
