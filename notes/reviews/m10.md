  intervals. It is gone (DECISIONS #40).

- `autosummary_generate` did nothing; it is gone with its extension.

## Answered, not changed

- **A parallel docs build** (efficiency): `-j 8` took 55 s against 128 s, but needs the
  jupyter-cache database created first, and `-j 4` with execution forced failed with
  kernel socket errors in both trials. Two minutes of CI is cheaper than a flaky job.
- **64 replicates per kernel in the gradients tutorial, or 20 dimensions** (efficiency):
  5.6 s or 18 s of 29 s. The implementation plan names 50 dimensions, and fewer
  replicates make the kernel comparison noisier; the page runs in its budget.
- **Local intersphinx inventories** (efficiency): an outage of docs.python.org, numpy.org
  or scipy.org fails the strict build. Committed inventories go stale; a rerun after the
  outage is cheaper.
- **tqdm in the docs group** (efficiency): CI shows the plain progress lines, local
  builds tqdm's bar. Both are what a user sees in that environment.
- **A paths filter on the docs job** (efficiency): 11 of 63 commits touched no docs
  input. Low value.
- **Deferred to M11** (altitude): `plot_meeting_times` for arrays of pilot meeting times,
  which two tutorials draw by hand; the tuning and pilot evaluations in `Result`, which
  `n_log_prob` leaves out; `Result.map` or functions in `quantile`, `interval` and
  `histogram`, for derived quantities; docstrings that cite spec IDs rather than the
  theory page.
- **BibTeX** (altitude): the citing page gives full references and `CITATION.cff`; BibTeX
  entries for the papers would need checking against the journals.

## `/code-review`

Ten findings, all fixed:

- **A kernel of one's own could not be tuned inside a mixture.** The guide's interface is
  to override `tune`, but `Mixture` called each component's private `_tuned`, which a
  custom kernel lacks. `Mixture.tune` now calls each component's `tune` with the shared
  pilot; a test tunes such a kernel in a mixture.
- **A censored pair past the slow-pair threshold raised no `slow` warning** from
  `issues` or `check`, while the live warning counted it. `issues` now counts a censored
  pair as unmet at `max_iter`; a test.
- **`summary()` computed the diagnostics twice**, once itself and once through
  `issues`. They are computed once per result and cached, and `diagnostics()` returns a
  copy; a test that changing the copy leaves the result's own.
- **Docstrings:** `sample` described tuning and the pilot as unconditional; the
  `diagnostics()` docstring did not say that `variance_share_of` is `None` below two
  replicates; twelve cross-references in docstrings resolved to nothing. The build is now
  nitpicky, so that a broken reference fails it, with eight type hints that have no page
  exempted (DECISIONS #43). `CoupledSampler` documents its attributes.
- **The citing page** missed the works that docstrings cite (Haario, Saksman & Tamminen
  2001; Andrieu & Thoms 2008; Craiu, Rosenthal & Yang 2009; Hoffman & Gelman 2014;
  Beskos et al. 2013; Gerber & Lee 2020; Thorisson 2000; Neal 2003; Hill 1975), and the
  automatic tuning among the methods to cite.
- **Pages:** the kernels guide's step-size scan counted half a pair's evaluations; Getting
  started repeated the settings guide's evaluation counts, now a link; the quickstart
  called its data the setting of emcee's tutorial, which has other coefficients and
  underestimated errors.
