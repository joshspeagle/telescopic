# M3 review: estimator weights, signed measures, the engine

**Diff:** 5b39221..f1ba2c1, about 800 added lines touching numerics: three `/simplify`
reviewers (reuse and simplification shared one, DECISIONS #3), on altitude, efficiency,
and reuse with simplification. The `/code-review` pass runs on M0 to M3 together.

## Outcome

- **The engine emits no warnings.** From a pool worker they would never reach the parent
  (measured under spawn and forkserver), and the result's `check()` re-derived the slow
  pair, so an in-process run warned twice. The engine now records the facts
  (`zero_density`, censoring as `inf`, evaluations, seconds) and passes a `Status` to a
  `monitor` after every iteration; `stop` and `slow_after` are gone. The sampler warns
  about slow pairs and zero-density starts (spec §6.3, plan §2.6).
- **Censored meeting times are `inf`**, as spec §6.3 and §7.1 state, instead of -1, which
  sorted first and hid a censored pilot quantile from the D-23 check.
- **The engine records evaluations and time** (R-EST-7), summed by `merge`, so counts
  survive pools and the shared `Target` of the pilots.
- **Covariances use one pooled product** for the mean second moment instead of an
  `(N, d²)` array per replicate: 15 GiB at d = 100 and N = 2 × 10⁵ before. Histograms
  use `SignedMeasure.mass`, a single `bincount` by cell.
- Simpler bookkeeping: `_Chunks` keeps the owner as its first column and takes its empty
  template once; finished replicates are a third `_Chunks`; `Replicates.correction` had
  no reader and is gone; `weighted_quantile` merges equal values with `np.unique` and
  `bincount`; the sample covariance is `np.cov`.
- Tests: a hand-computed signed quantile replaces a test that could not fail (four
  mutants of the projection had passed); a tagged kernel catches swapped chain outputs,
  which had passed every fast test; the validation errors are tested; the perfect-kernel,
  splitting-kernel, every-index and JOA20-anchor tests are gone, because the meeting-time,
  invariance and Hypothesis tests catch every mutant they caught. Nine engine mutants
  were all caught after the rewrite.

## Findings not adopted, or deferred

- Altitude: lanes and an abort for budget mode. Deferred to M7 with budget mode
  (should): `finish` will record the lane, and the monitor's answer will distinguish
  draining from cutting.
- Simplification: derive `SignedMeasure.n_replicates` from the atoms. Kept explicit: it
  stays right for a measure with replicates that have no atoms.
- Simplification and altitude disagreed on `Replicates.correction`: altitude wanted it
  kept for R-hat across the X-chains, a "should" of spec §7.3. It was removed; R-hat
  can record what it needs if it is implemented.
- Efficiency: pending at the time of this commit; its findings follow in a separate
  change.
