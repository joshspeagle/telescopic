# M4 and M5 review: results, diagnostics, tuning, the sampler

**Diff:** f1ba2c1..9a1ab63 restricted to `results`, `diagnostics`, `tuning`, `sampler`, the
kernels' `tune` methods and their tests, about 2,100 lines touching the public API: three
`/simplify` reviewers (DECISIONS #3), on altitude, efficiency, and reuse with
simplification. M4 had its `/code-review` with M0 to M3; M5's follows these fixes.

## Outcome

- **The tuning pilot did not converge for d ≥ 10** (efficiency reviewer). Each chain
  adapted its own covariance at the rate t^-0.6, so on N(0, I₁₀) the chains disagreed
  even from a stationary start (R-hat about 1.9), the sampler warned of several modes,
  and k came out far too large. The chains now share one running covariance at the rate
  1/(t + 1), started from the variances of 100 draws from π0; each keeps its own global
  scale, and a tuned random walk uses 2.38/√d (DECISIONS #7). k from 100 lag-1 pilot
  pairs, 3 seeds each:

  | Target, start | Before | After | Exact covariance |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | N(0, I₁₀), from N(3, I) | 778 to 1,900 | 138 to 153 * | 128 to 169 |
  | N(0, I₁₀), from N(0, 16 I) | 4,436 to 7,712 | 168 to 194 | 154 to 200 |
  | N(0, I₂₀), from N(3, I) | 40,630 to never met | 333 to 406 * | 274 to 360 |
  | N(0, I₂₀), from N(0, 16 I) | never met | 438 to 646 | 318 to 367 |
  | variances 10⁻⁴ to 10², d = 20, from N(0, 16 diag v) | not run | 438 to 646 | 318 to 367 |
  | N(0, I₅₀), from N(0, 16 I) | not run | 2,531 to 4,519 | 996 to 1,109 |

  \* With the shared covariance started from the identity, which π0's variances
  approximate here. "Not run": the old pilot takes hours there; the reviewer measured
  that it raises at d = 50. Started from the identity, the shared covariance gave 823 to
  6,551 on the badly scaled target and 16,518 to 32,745 at d = 50.

  The pilot costs 90 µs per step at d = 10, as before, and 130 µs at d = 50 (207
  before). At d = 50 the dense covariance from 4 × 10⁴ states is still noisy (its
  smallest eigenvalue was 0.01 to 0.18 of the truth): shrinkage or longer pilots are for
  M9's benchmarks. Setup: Intel Xeon @ 2.10 GHz, 4 cores (KVM), `threadpool_limits(1)`,
  Python 3.13.16, NumPy 2.5.4, SciPy 1.18.1.
- **Runs warn by default.** Spec §6.3 requires a warning for censored pairs, and none
  came: only `check()` and `summary()` reported them. Each run now ends by emitting the
  §7.4 issues of its cumulative result, except slow pairs and disagreeing tuning chains,
  which it emits as they happen. `issues` returns typed `Issue`s, and one helper
  attributes every warning to the first frame outside the package (DECISIONS #8).
- **Settings across runs.** With defaults of 1 and `None`, an explicit `thin=1` after
  `thin=2` was taken for "unchanged"; invalid first settings were stored before the
  engine refused them; `thin=0` raised `ZeroDivisionError` in the memory estimate. Now
  `thin` and `max_iter` default to `None` ("the first run's value"), settings are checked
  by `engine.check_settings` before the memory estimate, and kept once the first run
  succeeds (DECISIONS #8).
- **One cumulative `Replicates`.** The sampler kept every run's part beside a merged
  copy (111 MB against 58 MB after three runs, altitude reviewer) and advanced its
  indices by the request. It now keeps one cumulative `Replicates`, shared with the
  `Result`, and derives the next indices from it and from the pilot.
- **`Result.merge` refuses different seeds** (DECISIONS #9).
- **Faster results.** Per-replicate sums are one sparse product; `var` uses `einsum`;
  quantiles merge consecutive equal atoms (rejections repeat 74% of them) before
  sorting; the Clopper-Pearson bound is `binomtest(...).proportion_ci` and standard
  errors `stats.sem`. On 1.27 million atoms (d = 10, R = 1000), best of 3: `summary()`
  1.59 to 0.44 s, `check()` 0.41 to 0.08 s, three quantiles 0.78 to 0.24 s. Estimates
  are bitwise equal on a censored run of 300 replicates, except `var` and the
  Clopper-Pearson bound (within 4e-16).
- **Simpler code.** `Kernel.tune` holds the check and the pilot, and kernels implement
  `_tuned`; `choose_k` replaces `choose_kml`, whose `multiple` had no caller while the
  sampler re-derived the lag and m; a `Seed` alias; one slow-pair threshold; one dict of
  kernel names; `PilotRun` caches its statistics and lost its scales, `TuningReport` its
  `scale`, `adaptive_metropolis` its `n_chains`; `Summary` is a dataclass.
- **Engine and diagnostics.** `seconds` now includes the final join, 38% of a run at
  d = 50 (efficiency reviewer); `tv_bound` refuses infinite meeting times with advice.
- **Tests.** The tuning test compared a report with itself, and three mutants passed
  (reuse reviewer); it now checks the tuned kernels. New tests: the pilot on N(0, I₁₀)
  (the old pilot fails it with R-hat 2.74), the settings across runs, the censoring
  warning of a run, `tv_bound` with `inf`, merging different seeds. Sampler tests use
  k = 40, so their runs raise no "k may be too small" warning.

## Findings not adopted, or deferred

- Altitude: one validated `Settings` object for the engine, `Result` and `merge`, given to
  the sampler's constructor. The smaller alternative above fixes the measured faults
  without changing spec §8.1.
- Altitude: results that record their requested indices, so that `interrupted` is
  derived and a merge that completes a run restores exact unbiasedness: deferred to M7
  with interrupts (D-22).
- Altitude: `Mixture` names `max_weight_ratio` when merging reports. Merging report
  fields needs a rule per field (the largest ratio here); one field does not justify a
  general mechanism. Components share tuning streams: tuning randomness never enters an
  estimate.
- Efficiency: caching first and second moments across `summary()`'s calls. With the
  sparse product a pass costs about 20 ms, and quantiles dominate the 0.44 s.
- Efficiency: block reads of the pilot's normals and other per-step savings: the pooled
  pilot computes one Cholesky factor per step instead of four (timings above).
- Efficiency: the engine builds a `Status` every iteration (5 µs, 4% of a run at
  d = 10): M7 reworks the monitor for progress and interrupts.
- Efficiency: `Result` keeps `_replicates.owner` beside `measure.replicate` (7.6% of its
  memory): `merge` uses it.
- Efficiency, outside the diff: storing a repeated atom once, with summed weights,
  would cut memory and every pass about 3.8 times. It changes the engine; M9's
  benchmarks will weigh it.
- Reuse: `meeting_times(max_iter=)` has no caller. It is spec §6.3's `pilot_max_iter`.
